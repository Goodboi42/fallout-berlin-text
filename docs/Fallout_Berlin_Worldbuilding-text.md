## 1. Canon Positioning

**Fallout: Berlin** is set in **2287–2290**, parallel to the events of *Fallout 4*.

- Europe collapsed earlier than the United States due to the **Resource Wars**
- Nuclear exchange did affect Europe, but with:
  - Fewer direct hits
  - Greater long-term societal collapse
  - Heavy use of chemical weapons and proxy conflicts
- Fallout: London establishes precedent for non-US Fallout stories

Berlin is framed as a city:
> Broken before the bombs fell — and reshaped by them afterward.

**Playable Area:** ~10-15 km radius, abstracted into a layered world map (surface streets, U-Bahn tunnels, elevated ruins, green zones). Exploration via top-down 8-bit movement.

---

## 2. Pre-War Berlin (2040–2077)

### Political Context
- Germany exists within a fragile pan-European coalition
- Berlin is a **Special Administrative Zone**
  - High surveillance
  - Divided into corporate, military, and autonomous sectors
- Physical Berlin Wall removed, but replaced by:
  - Surveillance corridors
  - Restricted access zones
  - Corporate-controlled districts

### Technological Aesthetic
- Brutalist megastructures
- Analog computers and early AI
- CRT terminals, magnetic storage
- Heavy infrastructure reliance:
  - Rail (S-Bahn elevated lines as rooftops/perches)
  - Underground bunkers
  - Repurposed WWII facilities

**Visual Tone:**  
Cold, industrial, utilitarian — Fallout tech without Americana. 8-bit sprites: blocky concrete, flickering neon signs, rusted rails.

---

## 3. The Great War & Berlin’s Destruction

Berlin was **not fully annihilated**.

- 1–2 high-altitude nuclear detonations
- Severe EMP damage
- City-wide fires
- Chemical fallout drifting from other parts of Europe

### Consequences
- Large parts of the city remain structurally intact
- Radiation levels are uneven and weather-dependent (radstorms from Spree River)
- Underground areas offer relative safety
- Surface zones shift between livable and lethal
- Remnants of the Berlin Wall persist as psychological/physical dividers (East/West faction tensions)

---

## 4. Post-War Berlin: The City of Layers

Berlin is divided vertically and ideologically (echoing old East/West divide).

### 4.1 The Surface – *“The Ash City”*
- Ruined landmarks (explorable hubs with quests):
  | Location | Description | Faction Ties |
  |----------|-------------|--------------|
  | **Fernsehturm (TV Tower)** | Collapsed upper structure; active radio beacon for propaganda/pirate signals. Climbable for overviews/sniper quests. | Iron Ring broadcasts |
  | **Reichstag Ruins** | Shattered glass dome; labs, armories inside. | Iron Ring HQ |
  | **Brandenburg Gate** | Half-collapsed arch; neutral trading plaza, executions, markets. | Free Traders' Guild hub |
  | **Checkpoint Charlie** | Guarded crossing; smuggling standoffs. | Wall Breakers raids |
  | **East Side Gallery** | Fortified Wall remnant with faction murals/graffiti. | Border skirmishes |
  | **Potsdamer Platz Ruins** | Black market crater; caravan crossroads. | Free Traders' Guild |
  | **Museum Island** | Irradiated artifacts (e.g., Nefertiti bust MacGuffin). | Scavenger quests |

- Populated by: Raiders, scavengers, radiation-adapted mutants.

### 4.2 The Underground – *“Die Tiefe”*
The defining feature of Fallout: Berlin.

- U-Bahn and S-Bahn tunnels
- WWII bunkers
- Stasi facilities
- Corporate vaults and shelters

Tone:
> Claustrophobic, improvised, alive — Metro vibes with Fallout absurdity.

**Key Underground Sites:**
- Schattenvolk enclaves (ghoul info-traders)
- Flooded tunnels with "Spree Vault" access

### 4.3 Reclaimed Green Zones
- **Grunewald:** Mutated forest, dangerous wildlife (radhunde packs)
- **Tempelhofer Feld:** Massive communal settlement/airship docks/caravan routes. | Feld Commune HQ |
- **Spree River:** Toxic, biologically active ecosystem (fishing for mutfish)

## 5. Major Factions

Morally gray, joinable paths with conflicting quests. Satirize German history: efficiency, division, bureaucracy.

| Faction | Origins/Location | Ideology | Gameplay Focus | Visual 8-bit Style |
|---------|------------------|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| **Iron Ring (Eisenring)** | Former Stasi bunkers/Reichstag | Order through surveillance & engineering supremacy | Espionage, laser weapons, power armor (eagle motifs) | Blocky power armor, red eagle insignias |
| **Feld Commune (Rotes Kollektiv)** | Tempelhofer Feld (East lean) | Anti-tech collectivism/neo-communist equality | Hydroponic farms, pipe weapons, purges | Red banners, communal huts, massed infantry |
| **Werkbund (Freie Händlergilde)** | Industrial districts/Potsdamer Platz | Pragmatic scavenging, trade, rebuilding | Crafting, modular upgrades, black markets | Toolbox backpacks, merchant carts, neutral colors |
| **Bundesritter** | Medieval ruins (West lean) | Ritualized tech-reverence, hierarchy | Melee/laser knights, partial power armor | Spiked helms, chainmail over circuits |
| **Schattenvolk** | U-Bahn tunnels/sewers | Ghoul pragmatism, info-trading | Stealth quests, rad-resistance, feral threats | Glowing eyes, ragged cloaks |
| **Wall Breakers (Mauerbrecher)** | Wall remnants/no-man's-land | Chaotic anarchy, barrier-smashing | Temporary alliances, destruction quests (raiders) | Spiked mohawks, improvised explosives |

**Faction Interactions:** Alliances/shifts based on player choices (e.g., Iron Ring vs. Feld Commune border wars).

## 6. Vaults of Berlin

Vault-Tec operated in Europe discreetly.

Berlin vaults are:
- Smaller
- Highly experimental
- Co-developed with European governments

### Example Vaults
- **Vault B-12:** Generational memory manipulation (player origin hook?)
- **Vault U7:** Permanent social role assignments (bureaucracy satire)
- **Vault Spree:** Aquatic vault (catastrophic failure; flooded mutant lair)
- **Vault 101-D "Deutschland":** Player starting Vault; efficiency experiments gone wrong.

Vault Themes:
- Bureaucracy
- Social control
- Ethical extremism

**Player Origins:** Vault dweller (B-12/U7), wasteland orphan (Feld), escaped ghoul (Schattenvolk), Werkbund apprentice.

## 8. Unique Items & Flavor

- **Food/Chems:** Irradiated Bratwurst (heal + rads), Berliner Weisse (buff speed, rad resist), Pretzel Grenades (thrown explosives)
- **Weapons:** Gauss Rifles (precision), Laser MP (submachine), Pipe Mausers
- **Tech:** Armored Trabants (slow vehicles), Beer Hall Terminals (philosophy debates/mini-games)
- **Quirks:** Bureaucratic forms for quests, propaganda holotapes, "Ordnung muss sein" graffiti

## 9. Themes & Tone

### Core Themes
- Surveillance vs freedom
- Collective survival vs individuality
- Memory, guilt, and historical repetition
- East/West division reborn in new forms (Wall as eternal symbol)

### Humor Style
- Dry
- Dark
- Bureaucratic absurdity
- Broken propaganda and announcements

Example:
> “Attention citizens. Please remain calm. The evacuation has been postponed indefinitely. Thank you for your cooperation. Ordnung muss sein.”
